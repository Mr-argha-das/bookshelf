# The Search for Truth — Part II

70-second continuation of the teaser, 24 fps, H.264/AAC, 1920×1080 delivery from a 1280×720 animated composite. Seventeen shots move from an early ocean through conceptual life imagery, humans and civilization to philosophy, observation and humanity's place in the Universe.

## Reproduce

Install `requirements-teaser.txt`, then run `python film/part2.py`. `--stills` produces review images without encoding. All required plates and approved narration excerpts are in `part2-inputs/`. Original sound design and score are synthesized by the renderer. Edit timing is in `part2-edit.json`.

## Provenance and limits

AI-generated photographic plates with animated camera transforms, water displacement, mist, particles, light shafts and exposure transitions. This is **not live-action or generative moving-character footage**. No movie footage or soundtrack is used. Historical scenes are artistic reconstructions, not archaeological evidence. The underwater setting illustrates a hypothesis; the vesicles are conceptual imagery, not a verified first organism. Philosophy and science are presented as distinct approaches, not proof of a creator. Narration excerpts come from the user's approved final-script recording; the trailer does not reproduce every line of the full film.

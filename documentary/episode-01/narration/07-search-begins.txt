Alag alag civilizations ne... in sawaalon ke alag alag jawaab dhoondhe.

Kisi ne Gods mein jawaab dekha... kisi ne nature mein... kisi ne philosophy mein.

Ancient India mein... rishiyon ne existence, consciousness aur Brahman par chintan kiya.

Rigveda ke Nasadiya Sukta mein... creation ke origin par aise sawaal uthaye gaye... jinke jawaab ko lekar khud ek gehri uncertainty nazar aati hai.

Aur hazaaron saal baad... science ne isi Universe ko samajhne ke liye... telescopes banaye... equations likhi... aur space ki gehraiyon tak dekhna shuru kiya.

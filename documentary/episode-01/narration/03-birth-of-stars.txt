Waqt guzarta gaya.

Matter ikattha hua... gravity ne use apni taraf kheenchna shuru kiya.

Aur dheere dheere... pehle stars bane.

Un stars ke andar naye elements banne lage.

Kuch stars apni zindagi ke aakhir mein explode hue... aur unke andar bane elements space mein bikhar gaye.

Wahi elements... ek din naye stars... naye planets... aur shayad humare jaise jeevan ki buniyad banne wale the.

Socho...

Humare sharir mein maujood kuch atoms... kabhi kisi ancient star ke andar bane honge.

In a very real sense... humari kahani stars se judi hui hai.

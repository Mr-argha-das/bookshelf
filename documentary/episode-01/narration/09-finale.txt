Shayad hum is Universe ke liye bahut chhote hain...

Lekin hamare sawaal... is poore Universe jitne bade hain.

Aur isi liye... hum waqt mein peeche jaayenge.

Universe ki shuruaat se... Earth ke janm tak... life ke origin se... human evolution tak...

Ancient civilizations se... Hindu philosophy tak...

Aur phir... us ultimate reality ki talaash mein... jise kuch log God kehte hain... kuch Brahman... aur kuch... abhi bhi ek unanswered question.

Is safar mein... hum facts ko facts ki tarah dekhenge. Beliefs ko beliefs ki tarah samjhenge.

Na kisi sawaal se darenge... na kisi jawaab ko bina samjhe maanenge.

Kyunki ye sirf Universe ki kahani nahi hai...

Ye humari kahani hai.

Aur humari talaash abhi khatam nahi hui.

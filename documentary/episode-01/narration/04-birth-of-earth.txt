Lagbhag chaar point five four billion years pehle...

Space mein ghoomti dhool aur chattanon ke milne se... hamari Earth bani.

Shuruat mein ye woh khoobsurat blue planet nahi thi... jise hum aaj dekhte hain.

Ye ek garam... pighli hui... aur lagataar badalti duniya thi.

Lekin waqt ke saath... iski surface thandi hui.

Paani jama hua... oceans bane... aur Earth par aise conditions develop hone lage... jahan life ka janm mumkin ho sakta tha.

Lekin ek sawaal ab bhi baaki tha.

Bejaan matter se pehli zindagi aakhir bani kaise?

Science ke paas is sawaal par kai hypotheses hain... lekin abhi tak iska final answer nahi hai.

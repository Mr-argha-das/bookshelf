Agar hum waqt ko peeche le jaayein...

Hazaar saal... laakh saal... karodon saal...

Nahi...

Itna peeche... jahan na koi Earth thi... na Sun... na stars... aur na hi galaxies.

Lagbhag terah point aath billion years pehle... Universe ek behad garam aur dense state mein tha.

Phir space khud expand hone laga.

Aur isi expansion ke saath... shuru hui hamare Universe ki woh kahani... jise aaj hum Big Bang ke naam se jaante hain.
